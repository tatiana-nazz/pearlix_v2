# Overlay and Interaction Specification v2

Use a page for multi-step/high-context work (patient/invoice/Team/account, print); a drawer for inspect/act without losing list context (appointment, audit, X-ray metadata); a modal for focused confirm/create/edit that fits 560 px, or 720 px wide form. Current ad-hoc backdrop/dialog implementation is a defect.

All safe drawers/dialogs: visible labelled Close, Escape, safe outside click, focus trap, initial focus, and focus return to opener. Dirty forms suppress Escape/outside close and show a discard-confirmation dialog. Submitting forms and destructive confirmations do not outside-close/Escape-close; buttons retain width, show progress, and disable duplicate submission. Modal max-height is viewport minus 48 px with internal content scroll only; no nested list scroll trap. Backdrop is theme-aware; `aria-modal`, role, title/description linkage, and logical close placement are required. RTL mirrors drawer origin and directional actions, not close icon.
