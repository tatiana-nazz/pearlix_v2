# Stage 7 — Handoff Bill and Invoice Ledger

Stage 7 establishes Pearlix's authoritative financial hierarchy: Visit completion creates one Handoff/Bill, one Handoff has zero or many Invoices, and each Invoice is one completed payment receipt. Handoff—not Invoice—owns the total obligation, Patient, Treatment, Currency, Visit/Doctor context, OPEN/PARTIALLY_PAID/PAID/CANCELLED status, and backend-derived paid, remaining, and receipt count. The user-facing Payment entity and standalone Invoice creation are removed.

Doctor completion atomically saves notes, completes the Visit and Appointment, and creates exactly one OPEN Handoff with zero Invoices. Doctor cannot collect money. Staff may create a manual Bill, safely edit a Bill before financial history, cancel a zero-receipt Bill, or record payment from Handoff detail. Payment issue accepts only amount, optional date, and notes; Patient, Currency, Treatment, and Visit context derive from the locked Handoff. Overpayment and cross-Patient association are structurally rejected. Admin is supervisory/read-only.

Admin/Staff Billing has Overview, Handoffs, and Invoices. Overview combines compact Bill-status KPIs, separate SYP/USD outstanding totals, recent Bills, and recent payment Invoices. Handoff History retains OPEN, PARTIALLY_PAID, PAID, and CANCELLED records. Handoff Detail is the financial center with exact Patient/Visit/Appointment links, backend totals, safe Staff controls, and the Invoice receipt history. Invoice History is collection history; Invoice Detail and A4 print represent one immutable payment and link back to the current Bill context. Patient Billing is Handoff-first, with compact receipt history and fixed Patient context for Staff New Bill.

The safe migration converts legacy debt-style Invoices into Handoffs and legacy Payments into receipt Invoices. Manual legacy debt becomes a manual Handoff; visit-linked records reuse one Visit Handoff; imperfect legacy relationships retain explicit migration markers. Demo-owned records are rebuilt independently. Current summary/dashboard APIs derive debt from Handoffs and collection from Invoices without combining currencies.

React Query mutations invalidate Bill detail/list/summary, receipt list/summary, Patient Billing, related Visit/Appointment state, and role dashboards. Operational queries retain focus refresh and bounded refresh. Focused backend, frontend, migration, deterministic seed, and nine-scenario Chromium acceptance cover partial collection, multiple receipts, full settlement, manual Bills, Patient Billing, receipt print, Doctor completion, themes, RTL, and responsive layouts.
