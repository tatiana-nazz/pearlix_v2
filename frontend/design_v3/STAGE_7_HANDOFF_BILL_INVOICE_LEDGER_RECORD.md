# Stage 7 — Handoff Bill and Invoice Ledger

Stage 7 establishes Pearlix's authoritative financial hierarchy: Visit completion creates one Handoff/Bill, one Handoff has zero or many Invoices, and each Invoice is one completed payment receipt. Handoff—not Invoice—owns the total obligation, Patient, Treatment, Currency, Visit/Doctor context, OPEN/PARTIALLY_PAID/PAID/CANCELLED status, and backend-derived paid, remaining, and receipt count. The user-facing Payment entity and standalone Invoice creation are removed.

Doctor Active Visit completion atomically saves notes, completes the Visit and Appointment, and is the only current-workflow authority that creates a Bill: exactly one OPEN Handoff with zero Invoices. Doctor cannot collect money or create a Handoff directly from Billing. Staff cannot create, edit, or cancel Bills; Staff's sole Bill mutation is recording payment from an eligible existing Handoff. Payment issue accepts only amount, optional date, and notes; Patient, Currency, Treatment, and Visit context derive from the immutable Handoff. Overpayment and cross-Patient association are structurally rejected. Admin is supervisory/read-only.

Admin/Staff Billing has Overview, Handoffs, and Invoices. Overview combines compact Bill-status KPIs, separate SYP/USD outstanding totals, recent Bills, and recent payment Invoices without a New Bill action. Handoff History retains OPEN, PARTIALLY_PAID, PAID, and historical CANCELLED records. Handoff Detail is immutable financial context with exact Patient/Visit/Appointment links, backend totals, and Invoice receipt history; eligible Staff sees only Record Payment. Invoice History is collection history; Invoice Detail and A4 print represent one immutable payment and link back to the current Bill context. Patient Billing is read-only Handoff and receipt history with no financial create action.

The safe migration converts legacy debt-style Invoices into Handoffs and legacy Payments into receipt Invoices. Manual legacy debt becomes a manual Handoff; visit-linked records reuse one Visit Handoff; imperfect legacy relationships retain explicit migration markers. Demo-owned records are rebuilt independently. Current summary/dashboard APIs derive debt from Handoffs and collection from Invoices without combining currencies.

React Query Invoice issuance invalidates Bill detail/list/summary, receipt list/summary, Patient Billing, related Visit/Appointment state, and role dashboards. Operational queries retain focus refresh and bounded refresh. Focused backend, frontend, migration, deterministic seed, and Chromium acceptance cover visit-only Bill origin, partial collection, multiple receipts, full settlement, read-only Patient Billing, receipt print, and Doctor completion.
